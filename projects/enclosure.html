The brief

An independent enclosure sample with a separate base and lid, prepared so both parts print cleanly and close properly.

What I did

  • Modelled the base and lid with four alignment pins.
  • Exported STEP and STL positioned at Z = 0, and checked the meshes: no open boundaries, non-manifold edges or winding conflicts.
  • Set nominal clearances of 0.25 mm radially and 0.30 mm axially, with no CAD interference.
  • Showed how a broken, open mesh is regenerated from the retained CAD solid.

Digital geometry checks only; no physical print or measured fit is claimed.
