The brief

A noisy, misaligned 3D scan of a pump cover with missing areas had to become an editable CAD model.

What I did

  • Aligned the scan and fitted every feature: planes, cylinders, hole pattern and ribs.
  • Rounded fitted values to design intent (109.99 → Ø110).
  • Rebuilt a spreadsheet-driven parametric model.
  • Checked it against every scan point.
