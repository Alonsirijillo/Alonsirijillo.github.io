FEA-driven redesign: 25% less mass, 2.2× stiffer, safety factor 2.55.

The brief

A CNC aluminium bracket for a NEMA 23 motor under a 1500 N belt load had to lose mass without losing stiffness.

What I did

  • Compared three design iterations (solid, pocketed, optimized) in Fusion Simulation.
  • Verified the results in CalculiX: deflection agreed within 0.3 %.
  • Showed that a naive pocketed version dropped the safety factor to 1.57, while gussets and an R5 root raised it.
