The brief

A 3D-printed PETG enclosure for an 80 × 50 mm PCB with a lid that closes without screws.

What I did

  • Sized 4 cantilever hooks by calculation: 1.44 % strain (PETG allows 2.5 %), about 4 N push-on force per hook.
  • Added standoffs, a USB-C cut-out, an LED light hole and vent slots.
  • Checked zero interference between lid, base and PCB; both parts print without supports.
