Wheel, carbon-ceramic disc and caliper modelled and rendered in Fusion. It's the one spinning above.

The brief

A hero piece for this site: a motorsport-style wheel built and rendered from scratch to show real CAD and rendering work, not stock or AI imagery.

What I did

  • Modelled the tyre, a 5-spoke rim in matte black, a drilled carbon-ceramic disc and a red caliper as separate components.
  • Added custom branding: AC logo and "ENG" on the caliper, an F1-style centre-lock nut and "ENGINEERING" sidewall lettering.
  • Set up materials and rendered each component as its own layer, so the wheel and disc can spin on the web while the caliper stays fixed.
