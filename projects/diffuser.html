The brief

An independent academic CFD study of turbulent flow through a 2D diffuser.

What I did

  • Built and checked five cases: three meshes with SST wall functions, plus wall-resolved SST and Spalart-Allmaras.
  • Assessed flow-rate stability, mass balance, pressure recovery, wall shear and y+.
  • Post-processed in Python and documented numerical choices and limitations.
