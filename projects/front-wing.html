The brief

AF01 R5, an independent front wing and nose concept inspired by 2022 single-seater architecture, used to connect CAD geometry with a documented CFD study.

What I did

  • Modelled four wing elements, 12 separators and an integrated nose across a 2000 mm span.
  • Reviewed the geometry: one closed solid, 8 root connections, 24 separator contacts, 108 gap measurements and a STEP reimport check.
  • Ran steady incompressible RANS (k-omega SST) at 30 m/s with a moving ground, on three meshes (1.88 / 1.90 / 2.09 million cells).
  • Wrote a 44-page report on loads, residuals, continuity, y+, pressure, wall shear and wake.

Concept study: convergence, grid independence and experimental validation are not claimed.
