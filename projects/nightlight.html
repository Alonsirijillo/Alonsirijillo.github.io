The brief

A two-part housing for a plug-in night light with a PIR sensor, designed for injection molding.

What I did

  • Built a parametric model: 2 mm walls, 1° draft from the parting line, 1.2 mm ribs, cored bosses and pin towers.
  • Ran Fusion draft analysis and section checks.
  • Caught and fixed three molding problems before tooling: a reversed draft, a 5 mm thick plug pad and solid boss bases.
  • Rendered the product photorealistically in Fusion.
