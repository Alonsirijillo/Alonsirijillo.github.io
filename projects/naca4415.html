The brief

An independent academic CFD study of the NACA 4415 airfoil.

What I did

  • Ran eight steady RANS cases comparing Spalart-Allmaras and k-epsilon, three mesh resolutions, wall functions and wall-resolved flow at 0° and 5°.
  • Checked mesh quality, convergence and wall y+, and independently verified the aerodynamic forces.
  • Delivered lift/drag comparisons, pressure and wall-shear distributions and wake profiles.

Numerical results only; no experimental validation.
