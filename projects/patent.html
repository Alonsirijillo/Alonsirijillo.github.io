The brief

A full set of USPTO design patent drawings for an original travel mug, projected from one FreeCAD model so every view matches.

What I did

  • Produced the 7 required views: front, rear, sides, top, bottom and perspective.
  • Added surface shading on curved areas.
  • Drew a variant sheet with the body in broken lines to claim only the lid.
  • Laid out A4 sheets to USPTO rules: black line art, margins and FIG. labels.
