Heat-Set Insert Redesign

Bracket redesigned from screws tapped into plastic to brass heat-set inserts.

2 mmmin. wall

Tools

Deliverables

  • STEP, STL, 3MF
  • A3 drawing with insert callouts and install notes

The brief

Screws were tapped straight into a 3D-printed bracket, so the plastic threads stripped after one or two assemblies.

What I did

  • Redesigned all 6 mounting points for brass heat-set inserts (4× M3, 2× M4) with sized pockets, lead-in chamfers and a 2 mm minimum wall.
  • Kept the original footprint and hole pattern.
  • Added gussets and an inner fillet for stiffness.
