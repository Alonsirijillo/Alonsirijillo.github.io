The brief

BV SW-01, a self-initiated study under my own brand, Bajío Vettore: a formula-style steering wheel with 11 mechanical push buttons, taken from concept to a print-ready kit.

What I did

  • Developed the assembly: front fascia and rear tray, split left and right grips, a separate spring cup carrier, screw joints and alignment pins.
  • Designed a guided button module (piston, cap and spring cup) with a 2 mm travel envelope.
  • Prepared STL/3MF files and reviewed them in PrusaSlicer.
  • Ran digital checks of fit, tray insertion, button motion and tool access, and wrote an illustrated assembly guide with a one-button trial module.

Digital prototype: it has not been physically printed or tested yet.
