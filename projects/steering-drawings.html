The brief

The documentation side of the BV SW-01 steering wheel: a drawing package built from the R04 native model, so someone else could fabricate and assemble it.

What I did

  • Produced CAD-derived orthographic and isometric drawings, including a general arrangement and the corrected rear tray.
  • Compiled a 44-part bill of materials and a hardware schedule.
  • Added a button stack section with step-by-step assembly notes.
  • Documented the digital checks: part integrity, interference, button travel, tray insertion and tool access.

Scope: mechanical housing and controls only. Electronics and wheelbase interface are excluded; physical tests are pending.
