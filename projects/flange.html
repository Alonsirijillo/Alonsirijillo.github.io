The brief

An independent sample of the drawing packages I deliver: a round flange documented for manufacturing.

What I did

  • Modelled the flange: Ø72 mm outer diameter, Ø24 mm bore, 10 mm thick, four Ø6.6 mm holes on a Ø52 mm pitch circle.
  • Produced a dimensioned A4 landscape vector drawing in TechDraw.
  • Exported a STEP model and a dimension JSON, then reimported the STEP to confirm a valid closed solid with matching volume.

Personal sample with nominal demo geometry, not a paid client project.
